The only place that knows every step: a diagram file taken to a page or a picture in one call.

What belongs here is the steps as one call, the `diagram` command over it, and the end-to-end tests, which say what each type promises of a diagram taken through every step. Anything one step can do alone does not belong here, and neither does a diagram file.

Read this pile to find out what the pipeline promises from end to end, and how to draw a diagram from the command line. Change it when a step is added or removed, or a promise of the whole is decided again.
