The only place that knows takumi: a document rendered to a still or an animated picture, with no browser and nothing parsed.

What belongs here is how a document becomes pixels: its nodes built as the renderer's own, each frame of an animation posed from the document's tracks, the fonts registered, the scale, and each output format. Nothing that decides where an element goes or how it looks belongs here, and nothing that parses HTML, SVG or CSS.

Read this pile to find out which pictures can be made of a document, and how. Change it when takumi changes what it takes, or somebody asks for a format or a way of rendering.
