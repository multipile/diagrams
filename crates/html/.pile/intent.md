The only place that knows HTML: a document written as markup that loads and runs nothing, a whole page or one element to embed.

What belongs here is how each part of a document is said in markup and styles: its nodes, its animations, timed as a picture of the same document times them, the metadata of an element or a line as attributes a page's reader can find it by, and the fonts when the page carries them. Nothing that decides where an element goes or how it looks belongs here, and no script.

Read this pile to find out what a diagram's page holds, and how to embed it. Change it when whoever embeds the page asks for something it does not say, or browsers change what such a page may rely on.
