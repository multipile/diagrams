What is decided once for every crate: the toolchain they are built with and the check they must pass, so that no crate decides either alone.

What belongs here is the pinned toolchain and what the check checks, the workspace and its settings, the licence and the notices of what the diagrams adapt, and the prose that says how the steps fit together and how to work in them. Anything that serves only one crate does not belong here, and neither does anything that runs only on a pull request.

Read this pile to find out what a change has to pass, and how the steps fit together. Change it when the toolchain or the check is decided again, or a step is added to the pipeline.
